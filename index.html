01 — About Cynerva

Specialised in niche APIs. Built for global markets.

Cynerva Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. is a specialised API manufacturer focused on the development and commercialisation of niche and differentiated active pharmaceutical ingredients for global markets. Our portfolio spans complex and high-value molecules across diverse therapeutic areas, supported by strong process development capabilities, scientific expertise, and robust manufacturing infrastructure.
